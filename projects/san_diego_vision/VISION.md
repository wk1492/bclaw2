# Vision — San Diego Youth Sports Science

## Core idea

Give students access to their own performance data as the entry point
into STEM: biomechanics, statistics, computer vision, physiology, and
machine learning — anchored to something they already care about and
can physically feel.

## Why sports + STEM + personal data

Sports performance is one of the few domains where young people are
naturally motivated to measure themselves and iterate. A swing, a sprint,
a pitch — these produce real numbers with real consequences the student
can feel immediately. That feedback loop collapses the abstraction gap
that kills STEM interest in traditional classroom settings.

When the data belongs to the student — captured from their own body,
their own reps, their own improvement trajectory — the math is no longer
hypothetical. The question "what does this number mean?" becomes personal.

## What this is NOT

This is not a sports analytics product for professional teams.
This is not surveillance or performance gatekeeping.
This is not technology-for-technology's-sake.

It is a learning environment where the student is the scientist
and their body is the experiment.

## Target population

- Youth ages ~10–18
- San Diego initial geography
- Priority consideration for underserved youth (foster, homeless, YMCA-adjacent)
- Athletes with academic potential who are not yet engaged in STEM

## What success looks like (EXPLORATORY)

A student who came for the swing analysis stays for the math.
A student who came for the math stays for the biomechanics.
A coach who came for the data becomes a classroom collaborator.
A funding partner who came for the Padres connection stays for the outcomes.
